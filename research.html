Research interests

Questions that grew out of applied planning work and that I want to pursue through graduate research.

Landscape and land-cover change

Long-term landscape change reconstructed from historical aerial photography and satellite time series; land-use and land-cover dynamics in mountain and peri-urban settings.

Remote sensing and GeoAI

Machine-learning classification and change detection; GeoAI methods that scale from single studies to repeatable workflows.

Validated spatial modelling

Moving from area-based checks toward cell-level validation of land-change models, with explicit treatment of spatial uncertainty. My Dharan and Rakam Karnali studies show where this is needed.

BIM–GIS integration

Digital representations of infrastructure and landscapes, and how geospatial evidence can enter infrastructure models.

I plan to pursue an M.S. in remote sensing and GeoAI, with a Ph.D. as a possible next step. Research supervisors can find my applied modelling work in the Dharan case study.
