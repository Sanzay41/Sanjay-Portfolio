Civil Engineering

The engineering foundation under the maps: roads and corridors, structural design, surveys, estimation and documentation.

Practice areas

Engineering practice keeps my spatial work tied to what can actually be built.

Roads and infrastructure

Road alignment and corridor geometry in Civil 3D and Smart Road; transport master-plan inputs; road-corridor models for municipal planning; infrastructure BIM coordination.

Used in: BIM & GeoBIM

Structural engineering

Structural analysis and design in ETABS, SAP2000 and SAFE; reinforced-concrete detailing; building design and CAD documentation.

Survey and inspection

Drone surveys with the DJI Mavic 3E and Pix4D processing (orthomosaics, DSM and DEM); GPS data collection; bridge-site surveys and inspections; reinforcement verification and construction-quality documentation.

Estimation and documentation

Quantity and cost estimation; engineering drawings; construction documentation.

Water and hydrology

Training in gravity water-supply design and climate-resilient irrigation design (ICIMOD, 2023); DEM-based hydrology (flow accumulation, catchments, Topographic Wetness Index) applied in terrain and flood analysis.

Used in: flood susceptibility manuscript

Professional registration

Registered Civil Engineer, Nepal Engineering Council, No. 84549 (2024). B.E. in Civil Engineering, Pokhara University, 2023.
