Urban & Regional Planning

Risk-sensitive land use planning and new-town studies across Nepal. Each case study sets out the problem, data, method, results and their implications for planners.

Approach

Most of my planning analysis answers four questions in sequence, then turns the answers into map sheets and area tables.

  1. Where is growth heading?Land-cover change and a business-as-usual scenario
  2. Where can growth go?AHP-weighted suitability analysis
  3. Where must it not go?Forest, water, flood and landslide constraints
  4. Where should it concentrate?Urban nodes and growth areas
