Planning projects that start from the map.

Risk-sensitive land use planning and new-town studies across Nepal. Each case study explains the problem, data, method, results and what they mean for planners.

Approach

Trend, capability, constraint

Three questions that most of my planning analysis answers in sequence.

  1. Where is growth heading?Land-cover change, business as usual
  2. Where can growth go?AHP suitability
  3. Where must it not go?Forest, water, flood, landslide
  4. Where should it concentrate?Nodes and growth areas
  5. Which evidence?Maps and tables
  6. So what?Planning implications
