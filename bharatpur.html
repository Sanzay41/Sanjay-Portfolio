Why these maps

Existing land use, land-use zoning, land capability and settlement density together describe what the city is today, what its land can carry and where people concentrate. They form the evidence base for a risk-informed land use plan.

The assignment is ongoing, so this page presents map outputs only. Analytical findings will be added once the work is complete and the client permits publication.
