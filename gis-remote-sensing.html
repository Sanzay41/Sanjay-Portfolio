Turning imagery and terrain into planning decisions.

The strongest part of my practice: land-cover mapping, change modelling and spatial multi-criteria analysis, with ArcGIS Pro as my main platform.

Method

From raw imagery to a mapped decision

The workflow behind most of my projects.

  1. AcquireImagery, DEMs, vectors
  2. PrepareGeoreference, align, clean
  3. ClassifyLand-use and land-cover maps
  4. ModelChange, suitability, hazard
  5. ValidateCompare with observed data
  6. MapLegible, decision-ready sheets

Satellite imagery and change

Sentinel-2 and Landsat processing in Google Earth Engine, NDVI and NDWI indicators, multi-temporal land-cover mapping from historical Google Earth imagery, and Random Forest classification in ArcGIS Pro.

Terrain and hydrology

DEM processing, slope and aspect, flow accumulation, catchment delineation and Topographic Wetness Index, with soil-moisture-related indicators for environmental assessment.

Spatial modelling

AHP-based multi-criteria suitability, weighted overlay with hazard and natural-resource masks, and Cellular Automata–Markov land-change simulation in TerrSet.
