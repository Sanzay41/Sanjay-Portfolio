GIS & Remote Sensing

My established practice: land-cover mapping, land-change modelling, terrain analysis and multi-criteria suitability, mainly in ArcGIS Pro, TerrSet and Google Earth Engine.

Workflow

The sequence behind most of my projects, from raw data to a decision-ready map sheet.

  1. AcquireImagery, DEMs, national datasets, survey data
  2. PrepareGeoreference, project, align and clean
  3. ClassifyLand-use and land-cover maps
  4. ModelChange, suitability and hazard
  5. CheckCompare against observed maps
  6. MapLegible, decision-ready map sheets

Capabilities

Only workflows I have used in delivered projects or in the submitted manuscript are listed here.

Geospatial data acquisition

Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8/9 imagery through Google Earth Engine, including scripted bulk downloads; historical Google Earth imagery for multi-date land-cover mapping; SRTM 30 m DEM from USGS EarthExplorer; ALOS PALSAR data; forest, river and road data from the Department of Survey’s National Land Use Project; administrative boundaries; drone-survey orthomosaics and DEMs; field data collected with GPS and KoboToolbox.

Used in: Dharan, Bheriganga Valley, Rakam Karnali, Tehrathum reservoir thesis, flood susceptibility manuscript

Remote sensing

Image processing for Sentinel-2 and Landsat; spectral indices (NDVI, NDWI); multi-temporal land-cover classification from historical imagery; Random Forest land-cover classification in ArcGIS Pro; on-screen digitising of settlements, roads and rivers from recent imagery.

Used in: Dharan, Bheriganga Valley, Rakam Karnali

Spatial analysis

Slope and aspect from DEMs; flow accumulation, catchment delineation and Topographic Wetness Index; distance surfaces for roads, rivers, settlements and forest; kernel density of buildings; reclassification and spatial overlay.

Used in: Dharan, Bheriganga Valley, Bharatpur, Tehrathum reservoir thesis

Spatial modelling

AHP pairwise weighting with consistency checks; weighted-overlay suitability; constraint masking for forest, water, flood and landslide zones; Cellular Automata–Markov land-change simulation in TerrSet; AHP-based multi-criteria hazard susceptibility mapping.

Used in: Dharan, Bheriganga Valley, Rakam Karnali

Cartography and visualisation

Thematic and analytical map sheets with legends, scale bars and north arrows; map series for structure plans and land use plans; input and output maps for a research manuscript.

Used in: Bharatpur, Rakam Karnali, Tehrathum reservoir thesis, flood susceptibility manuscript
