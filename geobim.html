What I do now

Infrastructure BIM with survey data

At AMY METOM Engineering Consultancy I integrate drone-survey terrain models and imagery with existing conditions, designed road-corridor alignments and pipe networks produced in other design software. I assemble them in InfraWorks and run clash detection in Navisworks.

Before that I spent six months coordinating multidisciplinary building BIM in Revit for international projects: structural, architectural and MEP model review, clash detection and quality control.

Being straightforward about it. I have not yet delivered a full GeoBIM or digital-twin project. What I bring is both halves of the problem: a working GIS and remote-sensing practice and day-to-day BIM coordination. The goal is to join them properly.
