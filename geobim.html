BIM & GeoBIM

Infrastructure BIM coordination is my current professional work. GeoBIM and digital twins are developing specialisations, and this page keeps the two apart.

Current work: infrastructure BIM with survey data

At AMY METOM Engineering Consultancy I integrate drone-survey terrain models and imagery with existing conditions, designed road-corridor alignments and pipe networks produced in other design software. I assemble the models in Civil 3D and InfraWorks and run clash detection in Navisworks.

During my first six months in the role, from January 2026, I coordinated multidisciplinary building BIM for international projects in Revit: structural, architectural and MEP model review, clash detection and quality control.
