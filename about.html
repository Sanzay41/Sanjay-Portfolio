A civil engineer who got pulled toward the map.

I work where engineering, land use and spatial data meet.

Introduction

I am a registered civil engineer from Nepal. Over four years of consulting work I moved steadily from engineering drawings toward spatial questions: where a town can grow safely, how land cover has changed, and what the next thirty years could look like under current trends.

Today I split my time between two roles. In the evenings and on part-time projects I work as a geospatial analyst at Picasso Engineering Consultancy on land-use change modelling, risk-sensitive land use planning and new-town studies. In my full-time role at AMY METOM Engineering Consultancy I coordinate infrastructure BIM, bringing survey data and design models together.

I am preparing for graduate study in remote sensing and GeoAI, so I can make my modelling more rigorous, better validated and clearer about uncertainty.
