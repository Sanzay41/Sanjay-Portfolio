About Sanjay Kumar Yadav

Registered civil engineer in Lalitpur, Nepal, working where geospatial analysis, land use planning and infrastructure BIM meet.

I am a civil engineer registered with the Nepal Engineering Council (No. 84549), with more than four years of consulting experience in GIS and remote sensing for land use planning and disaster-risk assessment in Nepal.

My work has moved steadily from engineering drawings toward spatial questions: how land cover has changed, which land can safely take new growth, and what current trends imply for the next three decades. Those questions are answered with historical imagery, terrain data, multi-criteria analysis and land-change simulation, and they end in map sheets that planners can act on.

I currently hold two roles. As a full-time BIM Engineer Coordinator at AMY METOM Engineering Consultancy, I combine drone-survey terrain models and imagery with designed corridors and pipe networks, and coordinate the models in Navisworks. As a part-time Geospatial Analyst at Picasso Engineering Consultancy, I carry out land-use change modelling, suitability analysis and risk-sensitive land use planning for municipalities and new towns.

I am preparing for graduate study in remote sensing and GeoAI. The aim is to make land-change modelling more rigorous: validated at cell level, reproducible, and explicit about uncertainty.
